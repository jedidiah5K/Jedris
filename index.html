How to PlaySTACK · CLEAR · ATTACK

Pieces fall into a 10-wide well. Fill a horizontal row completely to clear it. Clear lines fast, and clear them with style: bigger clears send garbage to your opponent in versus.

Modes

40 LinesClear 40 lines as fast as possible. Your best time is saved.
BlitzScore as much as you can in 2 minutes. Gravity speeds up every 10 lines.
ZenNo timer, no game over. Topping out just wipes the board.
Online VersusReal-time 1v1 over the internet. Quick match, or share a room code with a friend.
Local VersusTwo players on one keyboard. Attacks become garbage for the other side. First to 2 rounds wins.

Techniques

  • T-Spin: rotate a T piece into a slot so that 3 of the 4 corners around its center are filled. If only one of the two corners it points toward is filled, it's a Mini.
  • Back-to-back: chain quads and T-spin clears without a plain single/double/triple in between for +1 attack each.
  • Combo: clear lines with consecutive pieces. The longer the chain, the more garbage you send.
  • Cancelling: incoming garbage waits in the red meter beside your board for half a second. Clear lines to cancel it before it rises.
  • Hold swaps the current piece into storage once per piece. Ghost shows where the piece will land.
